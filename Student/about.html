ABOUT SCCMS

Making campus problem-solving smarter.

SCCMS — Smart College Complaint Management System — is designed to create a simple, transparent and accountable way for students to raise concerns and follow them until resolution.

OUR PURPOSE

Turning complaints into action.

College complaints often get delayed because students don't know where to submit them or how to follow up.

SCCMS brings the complete process into one structured platform — from submission and assignment to tracking and resolution.

WHAT MATTERS

Built on three principles.

Every part of SCCMS focuses on making complaint management better.

◷

Transparency

⚡
